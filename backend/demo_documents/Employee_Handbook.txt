ACME CORPORATION
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024

================================================================================
SECTION 1: WELCOME AND INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

Welcome to ACME Corporation. This handbook outlines the policies, benefits, and 
expectations for all ACME employees. It is your responsibility to read, understand, 
and comply with the policies described herein.

This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Contract workers 
and consultants should refer to their respective agreements.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
================================================================================

2.1 Standard Working Hours

Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees 
are expected to be available and productive during core business hours: 10:00 AM 
to 4:00 PM.

2.2 Flexible Working Arrangements

Flexible working arrangements may be requested with at least 2 weeks' notice and 
require written approval from your direct manager and HR.

2.3 Attendance

Regular attendance is critical to business operations. If you are unable to attend 
work, you must notify your manager by 9:00 AM on the day of absence.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: ANNUAL LEAVE AND TIME OFF
================================================================================

3.1 Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year, accrued at 
1.67 days per month. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.

Unused leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar 
year. Leave in excess of this will be forfeited.

3.2 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical 
certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days.

3.3 Public Holidays

ACME observes all national public holidays. Employees required to work on a public 
holiday will receive compensatory time off.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: RESIGNATION AND TERMINATION
================================================================================

4.1 Notice Period

Employees who wish to resign must provide a minimum of 30 days written notice to 
their manager and HR. Senior managers and directors must provide 60 days notice.

4.2 Exit Procedures

Upon resignation or termination, employees must:
- Return all company property including devices, access cards, and documents
- Complete a formal handover with their manager
- Attend an exit interview with HR
- Have their accounts deactivated on the last working day

================================================================================
SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT
================================================================================

5.1 Professional Behavior

All employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally, treat colleagues 
with respect, and uphold ACME's values of integrity, excellence, and collaboration.

5.2 Disciplinary Action

Policy violations are addressed through a progressive disciplinary process:
- First violation: Verbal warning
- Second violation: Written warning
- Third violation: Final written warning
- Repeated policy violations may result in written warnings, suspension, or 
  termination depending on severity and frequency.

Serious violations including fraud, harassment, or data breaches may result in 
immediate termination without notice.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: EQUAL OPPORTUNITY AND ANTI-HARASSMENT
================================================================================

ACME is an equal opportunity employer. Discrimination or harassment based on race, 
gender, age, religion, disability, or any other protected characteristic is 
strictly prohibited.

Any employee who experiences or witnesses harassment should report it immediately 
to HR or use the anonymous reporting hotline at 1-800-ACME-EEO.

================================================================================
SECTION 7: CONFIDENTIALITY
================================================================================

Employees must maintain the confidentiality of proprietary business information, 
client data, and internal strategies during and after employment. Breach of 
confidentiality may result in legal action.
