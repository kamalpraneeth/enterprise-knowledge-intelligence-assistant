ACME CORPORATION
IT SECURITY AND ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Document Reference: ACME-IT-001
Classification: Internal Use Only
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

================================================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
================================================================================

This policy defines acceptable use of ACME's information technology resources 
and security requirements to protect company data, systems, and infrastructure.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: PASSWORD POLICY
================================================================================

2.1 Password Requirements

All passwords for ACME systems must comply with the following:
- Minimum length: 12 characters
- Must include: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters
- Must not contain the employee's name or username
- Must not be reused from the previous 12 passwords
- Must be changed every 90 days

2.2 Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for:
- Remote access to company systems
- Email and collaboration tools
- Financial systems
- HR and payroll systems

2.3 Password Management

Employees should use the company-approved password manager. Writing passwords on 
paper or storing them in plain text files is prohibited.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: DEVICE ACCEPTABLE USE
================================================================================

3.1 Company Devices

Company devices must be used primarily for business purposes. Prohibited activities:
- Accessing streaming services during work hours
- Social media use on company devices during work hours
- Installing unauthorized software without IT approval
- Connecting to unsecured public Wi-Fi without VPN
- Sharing company devices with family members or third parties

3.2 Personal Devices (BYOD)

Employees using personal devices for work must:
- Register the device with IT
- Install the company mobile device management (MDM) profile
- Enable device encryption and passcode

================================================================================
SECTION 4: DATA SECURITY
================================================================================

4.1 Data Classification

ACME data is classified as follows:
- Public: May be shared openly
- Internal: For employee use only
- Confidential: Restricted access, encryption required
- Restricted: Executive and need-to-know only

4.2 Handling Confidential Data

Confidential documents must be labeled, encrypted, and stored in approved secure 
systems only. Confidential data must not be stored on personal devices or in 
personal cloud storage.

4.3 Email Security

Company files and data must not be sent to personal email accounts. Only approved 
company email and collaboration tools may be used for transmitting business 
information.

4.4 Encryption

All confidential data in transit must use TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest on 
portable devices must be encrypted using AES-256.

================================================================================
SECTION 5: INCIDENT REPORTING
================================================================================

5.1 Phishing

Suspected phishing emails should be reported immediately to IT Security by 
forwarding to security@acme.com and not clicking any links. Do not attempt to 
respond to or engage with the suspected attacker.

5.2 Security Incidents

All security incidents including lost devices, unauthorized access, or suspicious 
activity must be reported to IT Security within 1 hour of discovery.

5.3 Reporting Channels

- Email: security@acme.com
- Helpdesk: Ticket category "Security Incident"
- Urgent hotline: ext. 9911 (24/7)

================================================================================
SECTION 6: SOFTWARE AND LICENSING
================================================================================

6.1 Approved Software

Only software approved by IT may be installed on company devices. A list of 
approved applications is maintained in the IT Service Catalog.

6.2 License Requests

Software license requests must be submitted through the IT service desk with 
business justification and manager approval. Unlicensed software may not be 
used on company systems.

6.3 Open Source

Open source software usage must be approved by IT and Legal. License 
compatibility must be verified before use in any company product.

================================================================================
END OF IT SECURITY POLICY
