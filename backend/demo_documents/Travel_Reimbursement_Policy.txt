ACME CORPORATION
TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Document Reference: ACME-FIN-003
Classification: Internal Use Only
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

================================================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE
================================================================================

This policy establishes guidelines for reimbursement of business travel expenses 
incurred by ACME Corporation employees. It applies to all employees traveling on 
approved business activities.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: TRAVEL APPROVAL
================================================================================

2.1 Domestic Travel

All domestic business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager 
at least 5 business days before departure.

2.2 International Travel

International business travel requires pre-approval from the department head and 
VP of Finance at least 2 weeks in advance. A detailed travel itinerary and 
business justification must be submitted with the request.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: AIR TRAVEL
================================================================================

3.1 Domestic Flights

Employees are reimbursed for economy class tickets only for domestic travel. 
Business class is not permitted for domestic flights regardless of flight duration.

3.2 International Flights

Business class may be approved for international flights exceeding 8 hours in 
duration. Approval must be obtained from the VP of Finance prior to booking.

3.3 Booking

Employees must book flights through the company's approved travel portal. 
Personal bookings will only be reimbursed if the portal was unavailable and 
pre-authorization was obtained.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: HOTEL ACCOMMODATION
================================================================================

4.1 Domestic Hotels

Hotel accommodation for domestic travel is reimbursed up to $150 per night. 
Employees should book using approved corporate rates where available.

4.2 International Hotels

International hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to $250 per night. Premium 
hotels require additional VP approval.

4.3 Receipts

Original hotel receipts must be submitted. Credit card statements alone are not 
accepted as proof of expense.

================================================================================
SECTION 5: MEAL EXPENSES
================================================================================

5.1 Daily Meal Allowance

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $50 per day with original 
receipts. This allowance covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined.

The daily breakdown is:
- Breakfast: up to $10
- Lunch: up to $15  
- Dinner: up to $25

5.2 Business Meals with Clients

Business meals with clients may exceed the standard allowance with prior manager 
approval. A list of attendees and business purpose must be documented.

5.3 Alcohol

Alcohol expenses are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: LOCAL TRANSPORTATION
================================================================================

6.1 Ground Transportation

Local transportation expenses including taxis, rideshare services, and public 
transit are reimbursable with receipts. Airport transfers are fully reimbursable.

6.2 Rental Cars

Car rentals must be pre-approved and employees should select economy class 
vehicles. GPS and insurance charges are reimbursable. Fuel costs are reimbursable 
with receipts.

6.3 Personal Vehicle

Personal vehicle use for business travel is reimbursed at the approved government 
mileage rate. Mileage must be logged in the expense system.

================================================================================
SECTION 7: EXPENSE REPORTING
================================================================================

7.1 Submission Timeline

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of completing travel. 
Late submissions may be declined at the discretion of Finance.

7.2 Required Documentation

All expense claims require:
- Original receipts (not photocopies)
- Purpose of expense
- Names of attendees (for group expenses)
- Manager approval

7.3 Processing Time

Approved expense reports are processed within 10 business days. Reimbursements 
are made via payroll or direct bank transfer.

================================================================================
END OF TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
================================================================================
